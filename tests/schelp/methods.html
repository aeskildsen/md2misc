Class Methods

Two methods.

.ar

.kr

Plays grains from a buffer.

Arguments:

bufnum

A mono buffer. Silence without one.

grainDur

The length of a grain. See SinOsc. Range: seconds

mode

The mode: a | b in code, and 1 | 2 in text. Range: 0 | 1

pan

> 0 is right, < 0 is left. Range: −1 to 1

Returns:

an array of two channels.

.new

Makes an instance.

Returns:

a new instance.

Discussion:

The instance plays nothing until play is called.
